About Xflow

Established in 2015, by identifying the demand for water efficiency and resilience in Cape Town, Sulaiman and his team at Xtreme Flow (Xflow) set out to become one of the leading water solution providers in the market for municipal and private clients.

Sulaiman worked for the City of Cape Town for over 20 years, and combined with the Xflow team's 40+ years of experience, they have become specialists in pressure management, bulk meter upgrades, sub-meter projects and other water-related work - evolving continuously into the Internet of Things water-solutions space.

Xflow has worked with reputable companies such as Faircape Group, Apex Innovation and JOAT, and completed work for the City of Cape Town.
