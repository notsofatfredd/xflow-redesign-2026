Metering technology

How water is measured.

Condensed from the current page's own technical copy - trimmed for scannability, nothing added.
01

Displacement (PD) Meters

Oscillating piston and nutating disc designs. The water physically displaces the measuring element in direct proportion to flow - accurate at low-to-moderate residential and small commercial rates.

5/8″ – 2″ · bronze / brass / plastic body
02

Velocity Meters

Measures flow velocity through a known internal capacity, converted to volume. Includes multi-jet, turbine, propeller and mag-meter variants - each suited to a different flow range.

Adjustment vane for calibration
03

Compound Meters

Two measuring elements plus a check valve - a turbine element for high flow, a multi-jet or PD element for low flow - for sites where demand swings widely.

Combined reading = total consumption
04

Electromagnetic (Mag) Meters

Uses Faraday's law of induction rather than mechanical parts - measures flow in either direction and handles raw or waste water without clogging risk.

No strainer required
05

Ultrasonic Meters

Doppler-effect or transit-time sound waves determine velocity; volume is calculated from a fixed cross-sectional area. Very high accuracy, low maintenance, long lifespan.

Flow-through or clamp-on designs
06

Prepaid Meters

User purchases water credit in advance via IC/RF card or keypad - credit is consumed as water flows, common in municipal cost-recovery contexts.

Card, RF or keypad interface
