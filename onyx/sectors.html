Sector A — Active, proven

Municipal

Xflow's municipal work is grounded in direct institutional experience. Founder Sulaiman Achmat worked for the City of Cape Town's own water systems for more than 20 years before establishing Xflow in 2015 — experience that shapes the firm's core specialisms of pressure management, bulk meter upgrades and sub-meter projects, delivered to municipal authorities across the Western Cape.

This is the sector with the strongest evidence behind it: a named, completed engagement with the City of Cape Town itself.
