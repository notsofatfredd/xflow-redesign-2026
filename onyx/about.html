About Xflow

Most contractors are hired to learn a network. Ours already ran one.

A registered Cape Town contractor built on 20 years of direct municipal water-system experience, now applied to municipal and private clients under one engineering standard.

Founder

Sulaiman Achmat

Established in 2015 after identifying the demand for water efficiency and resilience in Cape Town, Sulaiman Achmat and his team at Xtreme Flow — trading as Xflow — set out to become a leading water solutions provider for municipal and private clients.

Before founding the company, Sulaiman worked for the City of Cape Town's own water systems for more than 20 years. That experience, combined with 40+ years of experience across the wider Xflow team, shapes how the company approaches pressure management, bulk meter upgrades, sub-meter projects and the rest of its water-related work — including its ongoing move into Internet of Things water solutions.
