The founder

Twenty years on the inside, before founding the company on the outside.

Chamber construction, on site — Western Cape

Sulaiman Achmat worked for the City of Cape Town's own water systems for more than 20 years before founding Xtreme Flow, trading as Xflow, in 2015. He set out to meet a demand he understood directly: Cape Town's growing need for water efficiency and resilience.

The team he built since has 40+ years of combined experience between them, applied to pressure management, bulk meter upgrades, sub-meter projects and the wider civil and water infrastructure work municipal and private clients rely on.
