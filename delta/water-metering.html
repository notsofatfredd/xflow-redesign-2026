Water Metering

Six ways to measure what a network is actually using.

Accurate measurement is the starting point of every water-efficiency programme Xflow delivers, from residential displacement meters through to municipal-scale electromagnetic and ultrasonic systems.

Metering technology

The six methods Xflow specifies.

01

Displacement (PD) Meters

Oscillating piston and nutating disc designs. Water physically displaces the measuring element in direct proportion to flow, accurate at low-to-moderate residential and small commercial rates.

5/8″ – 2″ · bronze / brass / plastic body
02

Velocity Meters

Measures flow velocity through a known internal capacity, converted to volume. Includes multi-jet, turbine, propeller and mag-meter variants, each suited to a different flow range, with an adjustment vane for calibration.

Multi-Jet — ⅝″–2″, accurate at low flow, internal strainer standard
Turbine — 1-½″–12″+, straight-through path, large commercial & fire protection
Fire Meters — UL/FM-rated turbine variant for fire-protection requirements
Fire Hydrant — portable, aluminium, ~3″ capacity, construction & temporary use
03

Compound Meters

Two measuring elements plus a check valve — a turbine element for high flow, a multi-jet or PD element for low flow — for sites where demand swings widely between the two.

Combined reading = total consumption
04

Electromagnetic (Mag) Meters

Works on Faraday's law of induction rather than mechanical parts. Measures flow in either direction, and handles raw or waste water without clogging risk.

No strainer required
05

Ultrasonic Meters

Doppler-effect or transit-time sound waves determine velocity; volume is calculated from a fixed cross-sectional area. Very high accuracy, low maintenance and a long service life.

Flow-through or clamp-on designs
06

Prepaid Meters

Water credit is purchased in advance via an IC/RF card or keypad, and consumed as water flows. Common in municipal cost-recovery contexts.

Card, RF or keypad interface
