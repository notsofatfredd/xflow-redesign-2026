Sectors

One engineering standard, applied to three different clients.

Xflow works for municipal authorities and private developments today, and is building toward a third sector: IoT water solutions. The standard doesn't change between them.

01 · Municipal

Municipal

Xflow's municipal work is grounded in direct institutional experience. Founder Sulaiman Achmat worked for the City of Cape Town's own water systems for over 20 years before establishing Xflow in 2015. That background shapes the firm's core specialisms: pressure management, bulk meter upgrades and sub-meter projects, delivered to municipal authorities across the Western Cape.
