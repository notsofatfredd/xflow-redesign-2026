About Us

Twenty years inside the system, before a day spent outside it.

Xflow was founded by someone who already knew how Cape Town's water network was built, maintained and stretched — because he'd spent two decades doing exactly that from the inside.

Founder

Sulaiman Achmat

Sulaiman worked for the City of Cape Town's own water systems for over 20 years. In 2015, he identified a clear demand in the market: Cape Town needed water efficiency and resilience infrastructure delivered to a higher standard, and set out to build a company that could deliver it for both municipal and private clients.

That founding is what separates Xflow from a typical regional contractor. The team isn't learning a municipal network from a set of drawings; it's applying direct operating experience from inside one of South Africa's largest water utilities.

Since 2015, that founding experience has been built on by a wider team carrying 40+ years of combined experience across pressure management, bulk meter upgrades, sub-meter projects and related civil works.
