Company

Founded on the inside of the system it now serves.

Sulaiman Achmat worked for the City of Cape Town's own water systems for over 20 years before founding Xtreme Flow, trading as Xflow, in 2015. The company was set up to meet a specific, identified need: water efficiency and resilience infrastructure for a growing city. That grounding still shapes how the team works today, alongside 40+ years of combined experience across the wider team.
