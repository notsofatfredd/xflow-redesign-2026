Xtreme Flow was established in 2015 to bring practical water efficiency and civil engineering experience to the people responsible for real infrastructure.

20+

The foundation

More than two decades understanding how a city’s water moves.

Sulaiman worked for the City of Cape Town for over 20 years before establishing Xtreme Flow. Together with a team carrying more than 40 years of experience, that knowledge shapes every project: practical, grounded and built for the conditions around it.

Today, the company works across water efficiency, pressure management, bulk metering, sub-meter projects, pipe systems and civil engineering — while continuing to evolve IoT water solutions for a more resilient future.
