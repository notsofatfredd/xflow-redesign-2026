Sulaiman spent over 20 years working inside the City of Cape Town's own water systems before founding Xtreme Flow, trading as Xflow, in 2015. That tenure wasn't spent quoting the network from the outside — it was spent operating it, which is why pressure management, bulk meter upgrades and sub-meter work became the practice's founding specialisms rather than services added on later.

He founded Xflow to meet Cape Town's growing demand for water efficiency and resilience, setting out to build one of the region's leading water solution providers for municipal and private clients. The team he has since built carries a combined 40+ years of experience into every project.
