Water Metering

Six ways to measure water, matched to what's actually on site.

From residential displacement meters to municipal-scale turbine and electromagnetic systems, accurate measurement is the foundation of every water-efficiency programme Xflow delivers.

Metering Technology

How water is measured.

01

Displacement (PD) Meters

Oscillating piston and nutating disc designs. Water physically displaces the measuring element in direct proportion to flow — accurate at low-to-moderate residential and small commercial rates.

5/8″–2″ · Bronze / brass / plastic body
02

Velocity Meters

Measures flow velocity through a known internal capacity, converted to volume. Includes multi-jet, turbine, propeller and mag-meter variants, each suited to a different flow range, with an adjustment vane for calibration.

See subtypes below

Multi-Jet

⅝″–2″, accurate at low flow, internal strainer standard.

Turbine

1-½″–12″+, straight-through path, standard for large commercial & fire protection.

Fire Meters

UL/FM-rated turbine variant for fire-protection flow requirements.

Fire Hydrant

Portable, aluminium, ~3″ capacity, construction sites & temporary use.

03

Compound Meters

Two measuring elements plus a check valve — a turbine element for high flow, a multi-jet or PD element for low flow — for sites where demand swings widely.

Combined reading = total consumption
04

Electromagnetic (Mag) Meters

Uses Faraday's law of induction rather than mechanical parts. Measures flow in either direction and handles raw or waste water without clogging risk.

No strainer required
05

Ultrasonic Meters

Doppler-effect or transit-time sound waves determine velocity; volume is calculated from a fixed cross-sectional area. Very high accuracy, low maintenance, long lifespan.

Flow-through or clamp-on designs
06

Prepaid Meters

Users purchase water credit in advance via IC/RF card or keypad; credit is consumed as water flows. Common in municipal cost-recovery contexts.

Card, RF or keypad interface
